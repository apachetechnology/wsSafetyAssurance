G1: Ensure the DeepMind Machine Learning System is Interpretable
G1.1: Ensure transparency in the machine learning model
G1.2: Ensure the model's decision process is understandable
G1.3: Perform third-party audits to verify model interpretability
G1.4: Benchmark model interpretability against industry standards
S1: Demonstrate interpretability through model transparency and understandability
S2: Validate interpretability through external verification
C1: Definition of interpretability in context of machine learning
C2: Users for the interpretability include both internal developers and external regulators
A1: Interpretability encompasses both the ability to trace model decisions and the clarity of the model’s operating principles to a human expert
A2: Both user groups require understanding the model to different extents and for different purposes
E1.1.1: Documentation of model architecture and parameters
E1.1.2: Documentation of the data flow and processing within the model
E1.2.1: Existence of a feature importance report for model predictions
E1.2.2: User studies validating the understandability of model outputs
E1.3.1: Third-party audit reports assessing model transparency and understandability
E1.4.1: Comparison report of interpretability with leading industry standards
